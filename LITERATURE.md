# Literature

Sources cited in this research, by strategy family. Each claim is a short paraphrase, not a finding of this repository.

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| A | Peer-reviewed |
| B | Working paper, preprint or technical book with data or method |
| C | Practitioner material, no formal test |

`Checked` means the citation was looked up during the release. `Standard` means a well-known reference given in its usual form and not looked up again. Sources are given as citations, without links. A grade describes the source type, not whether its claim holds on gold.

## Strategy families by published support

From a July 2026 evidence review, restated here. Strongest first:

| Family | Published support | Usable on spot gold bars |
|---|---|---|
| Order-flow imbalance | Strong peer-reviewed base | No. Needs depth data |
| Options gamma and dealer hedging | Strong and growing | No. Needs options data, and the gold effect is an inference |
| Liquidity sweeps at levels | The narrative has none. Stop clustering itself is documented by Osler | Yes |
| RSI divergence | Weak and mixed, small samples | Yes |
| Quarterly Theory, SSMT | None for the named method. Generic intraday seasonality only | Yes |
| Malaysian SNR | None found | Partly. Pattern definitions are discretionary |

ICT and SMC concepts have no peer-reviewed out-of-sample evidence as named methods. This research tested several of them and none survived.
