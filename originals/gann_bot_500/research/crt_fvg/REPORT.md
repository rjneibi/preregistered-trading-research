# CRT-FVG-1: CRT + HTF FVG backtest report

Data: Dukascopy M1 2013-01 to 2026-10. IS = first 70% (to 2022-09-06); OOS = the rest, opened once after the IS selection was frozen (`results/selection.json`). 6480 IS backtests (108 configs × 3 entry TFs × 5 TP modes × 4 symbols). Costs: XAU $0.25 + $7/lot, EURUSD 0.5 pip + $7, GBPUSD 1.0 pip + $7, NAS100 1.5 pt; 1 tick slippage on entries and stops; 1% risk per trade, $100k.

## Verdict

**NO EDGE: 0 of 15 pooled cells pass all six gates.**

- The edge does not survive costs or out-of-sample. Every one of the 15 cells is negative OOS after costs (−0.07R to −0.64R per trade). Even the best of 108 settings was negative in-sample in every cell.
- Before costs the rules are no better than a coin, and slightly worse: in 12 of 15 cells, gross OOS avg R is below a random-direction twin on the same entries (gross range −0.41R to +0.49R). The only positive gross cell (5m, liquidity target) depends on a single +23.6R trade. No cell beats the random-direction twin after correcting for 15 tests.
- Costs are the killer on the short entry timeframes. A 5m stop is about $2.5 on gold and 2.5-3.5 pips on FX, so spread + commission takes 13% (gold) to 40-60% (FX) of 1R per trade.
- The two gold cells that are positive OOS (5m liq +0.35R n29, 3m 3R +0.25R n12) are noise. The 5m one lost −0.23R on gold in-sample.
- Prop-firm check: no cell reaches +10% within 30 days in more than 2.3% of windows. At 1-2 trades a month that is the expected outcome.
- Every walk-forward is negative (−0.13R to −0.39R). In the main 5m/2R cell, 12 of 14 years are negative.
